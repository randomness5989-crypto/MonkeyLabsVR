1. Introduction

Monkey Labs VR ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how that information may be used, and how third-party services used by our games may process information.

This Privacy Policy applies to all Monkey Labs VR games and applications, including VRC1, VRC2, VRC3, and VRC4.

By using a Monkey Labs VR game or service, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the game and features you use, we may process information such as:

  • Player or platform identifiers
  • In-game display names
  • Game account information
  • Gameplay-related information
  • Multiplayer information
  • Technical information required to operate the game
  • Information required to troubleshoot errors or problems

We only use information that is reasonably necessary to provide and maintain our games and services.

3. Third-Party Services

Monkey Labs VR games may use third-party services to provide certain features.

These services may include:

  • Photon PUN 2 - used for multiplayer networking and communication between players.
  • PlayFab - may be used for player accounts, player data, statistics, inventories, or other online game services.
  • Meta / Oculus Platform SDK - may be used to provide virtual reality platform functionality and platform-related features.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms of service.

4. Multiplayer Features

Our games may contain online multiplayer features. When using multiplayer functionality, certain information may be transmitted through our networking services.

Depending on the game, other players may be able to see information such as:

  • Your in-game display name
  • Your player identifier or related game information
  • Gameplay actions and information visible during multiplayer

5. How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing game functionality
  • Connecting players in multiplayer games
  • Maintaining player accounts
  • Saving game-related information
  • Providing online services
  • Preventing cheating, abuse, or misuse
  • Investigating technical problems
  • Improving our games and services
  • Maintaining the security of our services

6. Information Sharing

Monkey Labs VR does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services when those services are necessary to operate our games.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect our services, or protect the safety and security of users.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information used by our games and services. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

If you believe that personal information has been collected improperly, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action where required.
