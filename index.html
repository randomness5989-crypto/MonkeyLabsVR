PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Monkey Labs VR may process information required to provide game functionality and online services. Depending on the game and features you use, this may include platform identifiers, player identifiers, display names, gameplay information, and technical information.

2. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Our games may use third-party services including Photon PUN 2 for multiplayer networking, PlayFab for online player services, and the Meta/Oculus platform SDK for virtual reality functionality.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

3. MULTIPLAYER INFORMATION

When using multiplayer features, certain information may be transmitted through our networking services. Other players may be able to see information such as your in-game display name and gameplay-related data.

4. HOW INFORMATION IS USED

Information may be used to provide game functionality, connect players, maintain online services, save game-related information, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and improve our games.

5. INFORMATION SHARING

Monkey Labs VR does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services that are necessary for the operation of our games.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect our services and users.

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our games are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that information has been collected improperly, please contact us so that we can review the request.

7. DATA REQUESTS

If you have questions about information associated with your game account or would like to make a privacy-related request, contact us using the email address below.

8. POLICY CHANGES

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our games, services, or legal requirements change. The current version will always be available on this website.
