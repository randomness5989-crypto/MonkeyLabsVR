Monkey Labs VR — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Monkey Labs VR ("Monkey Labs VR", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be handled when you play Monkey Labs VR and how that information is used.

Information We Handle

Depending on how you use the game, Monkey Labs VR may handle information such as:

  • Game account or player identifiers used to provide online features.
  • Game progress, settings, inventory, statistics, or other game data that we choose to save.
  • Your in-game name or other information you make visible to other players.
  • Technical information needed to connect players to online multiplayer services.
  • Information provided by platform services when required for authentication or entitlement checks.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate Monkey Labs VR, provide multiplayer functionality, save game progress, authenticate players when required, maintain game security, troubleshoot problems, and improve the game.

Third-Party Services

Photon PUN 2

Monkey Labs VR uses Photon PUN 2 for online multiplayer networking. Photon may process information needed to establish and operate multiplayer sessions, including player identifiers and connection/session information. Photon documentation explains that its service can assign anonymous user IDs for sessions and can also work with external authentication such as PlayFab.

PlayFab

Monkey Labs VR uses PlayFab for game backend features. Depending on the features enabled by the game, PlayFab may store player identifiers, game progress, settings, statistics, inventory, or other game data associated with a player. We only use the PlayFab features needed to operate the game.

Meta / Oculus Platform

Monkey Labs VR uses the Meta/Oculus SDK and platform services. Information made available to the game by Meta may be processed as necessary to provide platform features, authentication, entitlement checks, or other functionality enabled in the game. Meta's own privacy practices also apply to information processed by Meta.

Information We Do Not Intend to Collect

Monkey Labs VR does not intentionally request sensitive personal information such as government identification numbers, passwords for unrelated services, or precise physical addresses through the game.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by the third-party services listed above when necessary to provide the game's online and platform features. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the game, its users, or our rights.

Data Retention

Information is kept only for as long as it is reasonably needed to operate the game, provide requested features, maintain records, or meet legal and security requirements. Retention periods may also be affected by the policies of third-party services used by the game.
